TERRAIN AND ORTHOPHOTOS

National Land Survey of Finland

© National Land Survey of Finland. The optional terrain packages use its open data: Elevation Model 2 m and Colour Orthophotos. Each package records its retrieval date in terrain.json; orthophoto capture years can vary by area. The data is downsampled, cropped, tiled, and recompressed, and water masks are derived from the imagery. CC BY 4.0.
